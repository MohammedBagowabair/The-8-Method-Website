The method

The spa, brought home.

You know the feeling. Warm stone, low light, the hush of a treatment room, and an hour where nobody needs anything from you. Then you drive home, and it's gone by dinner.

The 8 Method is an online program that teaches you to create that feeling yourself, every day, with what you already have. Each week opens one pillar of spa wellbeing, with short guided rituals you can follow from your bathroom, your bedroom or the corner of your living room.

Made for you, if…

No gym, no guru, no cupboard full of new products. Just a calmer way to look after yourself, a few minutes at a time.

The 8 pillars

Eight pillars, eight weeks.

Each pillar is a different way the spa looks after you. Over eight weeks they build, one on the next, into a daily ritual that's yours to keep.

  1. 01Week 1

    Breath

    Slow, guided breathwork that settles your body and opens every ritual.

  2. 02Week 2

    Water

    Warm baths, cool rinses and hydration habits borrowed from the bathhouse.

  3. 03Week 3

    Skin

    An at-home facial ritual with massage, gua sha and the products you already own.

  4. 04Week 4

    Touch

    Self-massage and gentle strokes for the places you hold tension.

  5. 05Week 5

    Movement

    Soft, mobility-led flows that loosen the body without breaking a sweat.

  6. 06Week 6

    Nourish

    Spa-inspired teas and simple food rituals that make self-care taste good.

  7. 07Week 7

    Rest

    An evening wind-down that turns the hour before bed into a ritual, not a scroll.

  8. 08Week 8

    Stillness

    Quiet practices that carry the calm of the treatment room into ordinary days.

Do I need any special equipment?
No. Every ritual is designed for an ordinary home: a bath or shower, a towel, a quiet corner and a few minutes. Optional tools, like a gua sha stone or a body oil, are suggested, never required.
How much time does it take?
Rituals start at just eight minutes a day. Take longer when you have the time, and keep it short when you don't. It's designed to fit around real life.
I'm a complete beginner. Is it for me?
Absolutely. Nothing is extreme or complicated. Each ritual is explained step by step, and you can go at your own pace.
